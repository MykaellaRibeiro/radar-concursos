# Radar Concursos design system

## Direction

“Radar editorial” combines the composure of a trusted research desk with the spatial cue of a scanning radar. The interface is mostly white and graphite; purple identifies active navigation, verified actions and the live scan line. Density comes from typography and rules, not nested card stacks.

## Tokens

- Ink `#101114`; muted `#686b82`; secondary `#9497a9`.
- Signal `#7132f5`; signal dark `#5741d8`; signal deep `#5b1ecf`.
- Surface `#f7f7f9`; border `#dedee5`; success `#149e61`; warning `#d98c10`; danger `#d14343`.
- Inter for interface copy; JetBrains Mono only for dates, money and tabular numbers.
- Corners use 12px for controls and 16px for major surfaces. Pills are limited to compact status badges.

## Layout

Desktop uses a fixed 264px sidebar, a lean 72px header and a fluid content canvas. Mobile uses a sticky top bar and a horizontal section navigator. Major pages lead with one strong information field, followed by ruled lists and tables.

## Signature

Movement lists use a vertical signal rail. The active point and soft sweep echo a radar without literal maps, rings or decorative sci-fi chrome.

## Interaction

Motion is limited to the initial radar sweep and direct state transitions. Focus rings are explicit. Tables scroll horizontally; mobile list rows become stacked records.
