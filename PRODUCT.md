# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase Auth/PostgreSQL/Storage and Vercel. Heavy collectors run outside Vercel.

## Users

People in Brazil preparing for public-service exams who currently monitor many fragmented official and specialist sources.

## Product Purpose

Radar Concursos centralizes discovery, verified status history, alerts, documents and, over time, exam intelligence. Success means a user can understand what changed and what deserves attention without checking ten websites.

## Positioning

The product combines a competition radar, a durable source-backed history and a future study-intelligence layer. It is not a news portal or a prep-course catalog.

## Operating Context

Users scan recent movements, filter competitions, follow selected opportunities, verify evidence and return as official milestones change.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Data comes through replaceable providers and keeps source provenance.
- Official evidence has the highest confidence; weaker evidence is never presented as certainty.
- Status changes append movements rather than erasing history.
- Optional providers must never prevent the application from starting.
- Heavy crawling, Playwright and PDF processing stay outside request handling.
- No competition facts, salaries, dates or statistics may be invented.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Radar Concursos. The voice is direct, calm and trustworthy. The visual direction is clean, technological and data-oriented, using the provided purple palette, Inter and JetBrains Mono. It must not resemble an old exam portal, government website, casino, crypto exchange or generic admin template.

## Evidence on Hand

No production competition dataset or commercial claims were provided. Development samples must be marked as MOCK and never mix with production data.

## Product Principles

- Evidence before urgency.
- History is a product feature, not an implementation detail.
- Show absence honestly instead of fabricating completeness.
- Keep collection providers replaceable.
- Make dense information calm and scannable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic markup, labeled inputs, adequate contrast and responsive layouts from 375px upward are required.
